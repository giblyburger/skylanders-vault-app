Quick compatibility rule

Figures generally work forward, not backward.

A physical figure works in its release game and later games. Game-specific hardware, vehicles, Traps, and Creation Crystals have additional rules shown on their individual cards.

  1. Find its release gameShown on every Vault card.
  2. Check “Works with”Exact supported games are listed.
  3. Use the matching PortalPortal requirements remain hardware-specific.

Installable offline app

Install Skylanders Vault

Apple puts web-app installation inside Safari’s Share menu. Installing creates a standalone Vault app with its own icon, full-screen layout, offline shell, and saved collection access.

  1. Open Safari’s Share menuTap the square icon with the upward arrow.
  2. Choose Add to Home ScreenThis is Apple’s install command for the app.
  3. Tap AddLaunch Skylanders Vault from its new app icon.
